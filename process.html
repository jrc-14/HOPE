Process

You may be wondering how to contact us, or how we build a website.

This page explains our process. We follow several steps to deliver a high-quality website that meets your needs.

From first call to launch, and beyond

Scroll sideways to see all ten steps.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We work closely with you to understand your business goals, target audience, and specific requirements for the website.

  2. 2

    Planning

    We create a detailed project plan that outlines the site structure, features, and timeline. It serves as a roadmap for development.

  3. 3

    Design

    Our design team creates attractive, user-friendly designs that match your brand identity. You review mockups and prototypes and give feedback.

  4. 4

    Development

    Our developers bring the designs to life using modern technologies and best practices. The site is responsive, fast, and secure.

  5. 5

    Testing

    Before launch, we test thoroughly for bugs: functionality, performance, and cross-browser compatibility.

  6. 6

    Launch

    Once the site is tested and approved, we launch it. We make the transition smooth and give you documentation and training.

  7. 7

    Maintenance

    After launch, we offer ongoing support to keep your site up to date, secure, and fast: regular updates, backups, and monitoring.

  8. 8

    Feedback and iteration

    We value your feedback and use it to keep improving our services and your website.

  9. 9

    Continuous improvement

    We keep up with the latest trends and technologies in web development, and refine our process to deliver innovative solutions.

  10. 10

    Client satisfaction

    Our ultimate goal is your satisfaction. We work with you throughout so that your vision is realized and you are happy with the result.

Note: Every steps (FEATURES) are not offered with every priced website package. It depends on the package you choose. We will discuss this with you in step 1, and confirm it in the project plan in step 2.

Now you are good to go!

How will we contact you?

The answer is pretty simple. Where our team is present, we will come to you in person. Where it is not, we will contact you through the channels you give us: email, phone, or social media.

We keep communication smooth and efficient, so you can reach us easily whenever you need help or have questions about your website.

How will you know we are genuine?

The answer is pretty simple. We will provide:

  • A portfolio of live websites you can open and test
  • Client references you can call or email
  • A written contract with scope, price, and timeline
  • Regular progress updates at every step

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

It depends on size and features. The project plan in step 2 gives you a clear timeline before work begins.

Can I change things after the design is approved?

Small changes are easy. Larger changes may affect the timeline or price, and we will tell you before we start.

What happens after launch?

You get documentation and training, plus ongoing maintenance: updates, backups, and monitoring.
